RouteDock the window with all settings; two of them, waveguide and metal.
RoutePlugin receives your mouse clicks and keys and runs the click mode.
create_route turns a computed path into the right cell in your layout.
via_needs decides where a via goes and which cut layer it uses.
attach_label puts a label inside its pad or GC cell so they move together.
use_profile switches the PDK settings when you change to a layout of another technology.

How a click flows through the files

An Auto-route between two device ports, from your click to the cell in the layout.

flowchart TD
  A(["Your click"]) --> B["gui.py RoutePlugin"]
  B --> P["profile.py settings of this technology"]
  B --> C["router.py route A to B"]
  C --> D{"Hits a device?"}
  D -->|yes| E["maze.py route around"]
  D -->|no| F["gui.py create_route"]
  E --> F
  F --> G["pcell.py Route cell or PDK cell"]
  G --> H["gc.py grating couplers and labels"]
  G --> I[["Editable route in your layout"]]
  H --> I
