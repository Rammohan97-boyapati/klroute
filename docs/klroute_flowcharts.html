klroute Flow Charts

What happens after each click, for every option of the Waveguide Route and Metal Route windows: the steps klroute takes, the dialogs it opens and the decisions it makes. Version 0.38.1.

your click or start what klroute does decision dialog you answer result placed in the layout

Settings and tools both

Where the PDK settings come from, and how you get to a click mode.

flowchart TD
  A(["Open or switch to a layout"]) --> B["Read built-in SiEPIC settings"]
  B --> C{"klroute_profile.json in your KLayout user folder?"}
  C -->|yes| D["Apply it"]
  C -->|no| E{"klroute_profile.json in the layout's technology folder?"}
  D --> E
  E -->|yes| F["Apply it (it wins)"]
  E -->|no| G["Settings ready: metals, via pairs, core layers, label position"]
  F --> G
  G --> H{"Which tool?"}
  H -->|"Ctrl+Shift+R"| I["Waveguide Route window"]
  H -->|"Ctrl+Shift+M"| J["Metal Route window"]
  I --> K{"Click mode"}
  J --> K
  K --> L["Path / Auto-route / Chain / Pin / Attach / Label / Bridge (metal)"]

What gets placed both

The decision made every time a route is finished (Path, Auto-route, Chain links, typed points and rulers).

flowchart TD
  A(["Route computed"]) --> B{"Create as?"}
  B -->|"Path / Polygon (fixed)"| C[["Plain shapes; waveguides get a cell with SiEPIC pins and DevRec"]]
  B -->|"Editable cell"| D{"Grating couplers wanted at a free end?"}
  D -->|yes| E[["klroute Route cell: route + GCs + opt_in / out_ labels"]]
  D -->|no| F{"Waveguide window with a Bend cell chosen?"}
  F -->|yes| G[["klroute Route cell built from PDK bend cells + straight cells"]]
  F -->|no| H{"PDK waveguide type and horizontal / vertical ends?"}
  H -->|yes| I[["PDK Waveguide cell"]]
  H -->|no| J{"Metal with a PDK wire cell (auto or chosen)?"}
  J -->|yes| K[["PDK wire cell + separate via cell where needed"]]
  J -->|no| L[["klroute Route cell (any layers, vias inside)"]]

Path mode both

Click every corner, like KLayout's Path tool. Backspace removes the last corner, Esc or right-click cancels.

flowchart TD
  A(["Click the first corner"]) --> B{"Within 12 px of a device port?"}
  B -->|yes| C["Snap onto the port; the route leaves along it"]
  B -->|no| D["Snap to the grid"]
  C --> E(["Click the next corner"])
  D --> E
  E --> F["Keep the segment Manhattan, 45 degree or any angle (Segments)"]
  F --> G{"Metal window: Shift+click or V?"}
  G -->|yes| H[/"Via dialog: next metal, via layer, sizes"/]
  H --> I["Mark a layer change at this corner"]
  I --> E
  G -->|no| J{"Clicked on a port?"}
  J -->|yes| L["Finish"]
  J -->|no| K{"Double-click or Enter?"}
  K -->|no| E
  K -->|yes| L
  L --> M[["Place the route: see What gets placed"]]

Auto-route mode both

Click A and B; klroute chooses the shape.

flowchart TD
  A(["Click A"]) --> B(["Click B"])
  B --> C["End directions: the port's direction, else Leave A / B towards"]
  C --> D{"A GC goes on an end and the PDK's DFT.xml fixes the GC orientation?"}
  D -->|yes| E["Force that end's direction so the GC faces the required way"]
  D -->|no| F
  E --> F["Try straight, L, Z, S-bend, U-turn and detours; keep the shortest that fits the bend radius"]
  F --> G{"Route around other devices on, and the route hits something?"}
  G -->|yes| H["Grid search: Manhattan path around DevRecs (waveguide) or other metal (metal), leaving each pin straight first"]
  G -->|no| I{"A route fits?"}
  H --> I
  I -->|no| J["Message: no route fits, move a component"]
  I -->|yes| K[["Place the route: see What gets placed"]]

Chain mode both

Click components one after another; each pair is linked with one route.

flowchart TD
  A(["Click the first component"]) --> B(["Click the next component"])
  B --> C{"Chain: ask ports... ticked?"}
  C -->|yes| D[/"Link dialog: PDK waveguide or wire cell, bend cell, port 1, port 2, labels, device ID"/]
  D -->|Cancel| S["Skip this link"]
  S --> B
  C -->|no| E["Pick free ports: shortest route that does not run through either component"]
  D -->|Connect| F["Use the chosen type and bend cell from now on"]
  F --> G["Route port to port, around devices if on"]
  E --> G
  G --> H[["Place the route: see What gets placed"]]
  H --> I{"Grating couplers in this link with a label?"}
  I -->|yes| J[["opt_in / out_ label, kept with the GC"]]
  I -->|no| K["Continue from the component just clicked"]
  J --> K
  K --> B
  B -. "double-click, Enter, Esc" .-> Z(["Chain ends; links made stay"])

Pin mode both

Add a SiEPIC pin to the current cell by clicking a waveguide end or a metal contact.

flowchart TD
  A(["Move the mouse"]) --> B{"Open waveguide end within the snap radius?"}
  B -->|yes| C["Outline the end"]
  B -->|no| D{"Metal contact under the mouse?"}
  D -->|yes| E["Outline the contact"]
  D -->|no| A
  C --> F(["Click"])
  E --> F
  F --> G{"Current cell is a PDK / library cell?"}
  G -->|yes| X["Refused: open your own cell first"]
  G -->|no| H{"A pin is already there?"}
  H -->|yes| Y["Refused"]
  H -->|no| I[/"Pin dialog: name (next optN / elecN), optical or electrical, direction, width"/]
  I --> J[["Pin on PinRec or PinRecM with its name, in the current cell"]]

Attach both

Put one component onto another's pin, without a route.

flowchart TD
  A(["Click the component to move"]) --> B(["Click the component to attach it to"])
  B --> C["List the pins of both: optical (waveguide window) or electrical (metal window); plain pads offer their edge middles"]
  C --> D[/"Pick the two pins (closest free pair suggested)"/]
  D --> E["Turn by 0, 90, 180 or 270 degrees so the pins face each other; shift so they meet; snap to grid"]
  E --> F["Collect what is attached to the moved part: parts on its pins, their labels"]
  F --> G["Move all of them"]
  G --> H{"A route has one end on the moved group?"}
  H -->|yes| I["Re-route that end; the far end stays"]
  H -->|no| J[["Done: one undo step"]]
  I --> J

Bridge mode metal

Let part of a metal route hop onto another metal, for example over a crossing wire.

flowchart TD
  A(["Click a point on a metal route"]) --> B["Snap onto the route's centre line"]
  B --> C(["Click a second point on the same route"])
  C --> D{"Both points between the route's ends?"}
  D -->|no| X["Message: click between the ends"]
  D -->|yes| E[/"Via dialog: bridge metal, via layer, block, cut, spacing, enclosure, exclusion"/]
  E --> F["Remove the old route"]
  F --> G[["Own metal, bridge metal, own metal"]]
  G --> H[["A via at both break points; one undo step"]]

Layer change while drawing metal

Path mode in the Metal window: Shift+click a corner, press V, or use the panel button.

flowchart TD
  A(["Shift+click a corner or press V"]) --> B{"Already a change at this corner?"}
  B -->|yes| C["Undo the change at this corner"]
  B -->|no| D[/"Via dialog: continue on metal, via layer (required), sizes, exclusion"/]
  D -->|Cancel| E["No change"]
  D -->|"Add via"| F["Status shows the order, for example M1 to M2"]
  F --> G(["Keep clicking corners; finish with a double-click"])
  G --> H[["One route piece per metal, each as a PDK wire cell or klroute Route cell"]]
  H --> I[["A via centred on every change point"]]

Connect selected device to GC array waveguide

One button: every free optical port gets its own grating coupler in a fiber-array column.

flowchart TD
  A(["Select a device, press Connect selected device"]) --> B["Find its free optical ports"]
  B --> C["Plan one GC column on the chosen side at the GC pitch"]
  C --> D["Order the ports so no routes cross; ports facing away escape around the device"]
  D --> E["Shift the column if a bend would not fit the radius"]
  E --> F[["One editable route cell per port, with its GC"]]
  F --> G[["Input port's GC: opt_in label; all others: out_ labels"]]
